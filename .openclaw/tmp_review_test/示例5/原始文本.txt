Immunosenescence and cancer: mechanisms and therapeutic
targets
ABSTRACT
Immunosenescence contributes to tumor progression. This review summarizes current knowledge on immune aging and
cancer immunotherapy.
Keywords: Immunosenescence; Tumor progression; Aging
Concept, process, and hallmarks
of immunosenescence
Immunosenescence is a multifactorial process. Its hallmarks include cell cycle arrest, telomere shortening and altered
signaling.
Immunosenescence and cancer
Generally, the risk of malignant tumors increases with age. In addition to the accumulation of genetic mutations, many
researchers believe that immunosenescence may also play an important role in the tumoral process.
