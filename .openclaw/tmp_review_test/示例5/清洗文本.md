# Immunosenescence and cancer: mechanisms and therapeutic targets

## Abstract

Immunosenescence contributes to tumor progression. This review summarizes current knowledge on immune aging and cancer immunotherapy.

**Keywords:** Immunosenescence; Tumor progression; Aging

---

## Concept, process, and hallmarks of immunosenescence

of immunosenescence Immunosenescence is a multifactorial process. Its hallmarks include cell cycle arrest, telomere shortening and altered signaling.

## Immunosenescence and cancer

Generally, the risk of malignant tumors increases with age. In addition to the accumulation of genetic mutations, many researchers believe that immunosenescence may also play an important role in the tumoral process. DeSantis et al. [49] found a higher risk of tumorigenesis in the elderly group. By analyzing the age distributions of 100 different tumors, Palmer et al. [24] concluded that the immune system may play an important role in tumor development.

## Conclusion

Immunosenescence is a key driver of tumor progression. Note: extra row
